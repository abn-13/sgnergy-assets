FAQ

Before we start.

Is my data safe?

Use the Free EUI check form for building and contact details. Email first to agree how meter data will be shared. Review /privacy before sending sensitive information.

Is this a statutory audit?

No. This is a screening report, not a PEA or MEI audit. Professional judgement belongs to the appropriate engineer.

What do I need to send?

Start with building and contact details at /free-eui-check. For a paid review, email the hourly export and floor area after agreeing the scope. The form is not a file-upload service.
